Insights

What I learned

Where the course was hardest, what you’ll keep doing, and the moment your thinking changed.

Friction

The hardest part for me was that I often couldn’t tie the exercises to real examples from my own work. That left me worried throughout about how much would stick and what I’d actually be able to apply later. What got me through was knowing I can come back to the material on my next project and use it as a template.

Key learnings

My biggest takeaways are how much golden datasets and eval gates matter, and that a failed gate doesn’t always mean stopping the project. I plan to use them as a guide all the way through my next projects.

Aha moment

My aha moment came right at the start. I realized that what I had been using in my work as “LLM-as-a-judge” didn’t have measurable, hard pass or hard fail criteria.
